# ShopIntel 📊

## E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analysis

ShopIntel is a data analytics project I built to understand how an e-commerce business is performing through its sales and customer data.

I used Python for data cleaning and analysis, RFM analysis to understand customer behavior, and Power BI to turn the findings into an interactive dashboard.

## What I Worked On

- Cleaned and prepared the e-commerce dataset
- Explored sales, revenue, products, categories, and customer data
- Analyzed monthly and category-wise revenue
- Identified top-performing products
- Performed RFM analysis for customer segmentation
- Studied customer segments and their behavior
- Analyzed payment methods and locations
- Created an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Converted the analysis into practical business recommendations

## Tools & Technologies

- Pandas

## Some Key Findings

During the analysis, I found that:

- Coffee Maker was among the top-performing products.
- Electronics was the leading category in terms of revenue.
- Delhi and New Delhi showed strong sales contribution.
- UPI was a commonly used payment method.
- Champion customers were an important high-value segment.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I suggested:

- Focusing more on high-performing products and categories
- Building loyalty strategies for Champion customers
- Using targeted offers for customers with lower engagement
- Personalizing marketing based on customer segments
- Keeping popular products well-stocked
- Continuing to support widely used digital payment methods
